OpenRecord is a studio working on ownership, authorship and the agreements that shape creative work.

Our focus is disputes and the structures that prevent them, built through research, writing and advisory work.

Creative work moves quickly and often informally, through deals made in conversation, agreements signed without full attention, and relationships that shift once money or credit enters the picture. What was understood at the outset does not always hold.

We study these situations closely, in writing and in practice. Our pieces examine ownership, authorship and the disputes that arise when creative relationships change. Our advisory work applies the same thinking to the agreements and conflicts creators and artists actually face.

Based in New Delhi. Working with clients across the globe.
