01 / Overview

OpenRecord is a legal practice advising creators and artists across India’s creative economy.

We work across contracts, disputes and legal advisory, with a focus on the commercial and legal structures behind creative work.

Creative work is increasingly commercial, collaborative and public. A brand deal, a collaboration, a licence, a piece of content or a creative partnership can carry consequences long after the work itself is finished.

OpenRecord exists to bring considered legal advice into those relationships. We work with creators and artists to understand what they are agreeing to, protect what they have built and navigate situations when relationships or agreements break down.

The practice is built around a simple idea: good legal work should make creative work easier to pursue, not harder. That means clear advice, careful drafting and a practical understanding of how creative businesses actually operate.

OpenRecord is based in New Delhi and works with clients across the globe.

The things we create deserve to last.
