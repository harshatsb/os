OpenRecord is a legal practice for creators and artists, with a focus on disputes arising from creative work.

We advise on disputes, contracts and the legal structures around creative work. Our work spans commercial relationships, ownership, rights and the agreements that shape them.

Creative work often sits within relationships that are commercial, collaborative and ongoing. When those relationships change, the legal position can become as important as the work itself.

OpenRecord advises creators and artists through these situations. We assess agreements, rights and obligations, help resolve disputes and provide clear advice on the decisions that follow. We also work on the contracts and legal structures that can prevent uncertainty before it becomes a dispute.

Based in New Delhi; working with clients across the globe.

When creative relationships change, the record matters.

PRACTICES

Disputes

Advice and representation in disputes involving agreements, ownership, creative work and commercial relationships.

Contracts

Drafting and review of agreements for collaborations, brand partnerships, licensing and other creative arrangements.

Advisory

Ongoing legal advice on rights, ownership, commercial relationships and the business of creative work.
